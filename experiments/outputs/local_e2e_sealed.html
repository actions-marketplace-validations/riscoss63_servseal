Method: the reference stores a 256-coordinate square-root sketch of the full next-token distribution at every probe position. A black-box endpoint exposes no distribution, so it is sampled one token at a time and two statistics are formed against those stored sketches: S1, the estimated Bhattacharyya coefficient of the empirical sample, which sees the tail; and S2, the rate at which the reference's most likely token comes back, which sees only the head. A serving filter pushes S2 up — cutting the tail concentrates the sample on the head — whereas changed weights push it down.

What SEALED does and does not say here. It says the sample fell inside the interval the unchanged reference produces at this budget. It is not proof of identity: what is guaranteed is the measured detection power at the budget spent, not certainty, and more tokens buy more power. top_p is deliberately never sent, because sending it would switch off the server-side filter this test exists to find.
