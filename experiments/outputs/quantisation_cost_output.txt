A flat row would mean you can read someone else's benchmark instead of
measuring your own model. No row is flat, and the two float rows are the
cleanest case: the same operation is an exact no-op on one model and a
measurable change on another.
Note which schemes vary most. Per-tensor int8 is the closest to flat,
because a single outlier weight sets the scale and every transformer has
one; the per-channel and group-wise schemes follow the model's own
structure, and that is what differs between models. The better the
scheme, the less its cost transfers.
